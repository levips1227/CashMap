# Loan Manager Reports

In **Loans → Loan Manager → Reports**, choose Monthly Statement, Interest Report, or Principal & Balance Report. The dashboard retains the statement preview and shows summary cards and a per-loan table for period reports. **Download PDF** saves a standalone report directly, without opening the browser print dialog or including the application navigation. PDF generation is imported with the reports dashboard to avoid click-time module loading failures.

- Monthly statements use the loan selected in the loan list and the chosen statement month.
- Interest and principal reports support one, several, or all loans via borrower/loan checkboxes, with year to date, last 12 months, calendar years, and custom dates. Both date boundaries are inclusive and apply to installment due dates for scheduled payments. Principal-only extras, draws, and originations use actual dates. Last 12 months ends today and starts the day after the same date last year (with leap-day handling).
- Interest paid and principal paid use recorded payment portions, excluding projections and escrow. The existing servicing engine identifies the installment each scheduled payment satisfies; this includes split, early, and late payments. For example, an October 1 installment paid September 30 is included in October. Report filtering, summaries, monthly breakdowns, and balances all use this applied-period basis. Recorded activity displays paid dates; installment assignments still determine report inclusion and totals. Principal-only extras keep their actual dates. Loans without a usable term/schedule fall back to recorded dates. Periods with no payments show zero.
- Net principal change equals new borrowing (original disbursements and draws) minus principal repaid. Negative change means debt decreased. Opening balance is immediately before the range and closing balance includes the final day, both on the applied-period basis (these can differ from posting-date balances). Adjustments are included when entered as draws or principal payment entries; there is no separate adjustment ledger. Missing origination dates are flagged because historical original balances cannot be assigned reliably.
- PDFs use the jsPDF/AutoTable direct-download generator, with selectable text, neutral statement-style tables, automatic pagination, and two-column statement information panels. No screenshot capture is used. The monthly statement preview retains its existing layout; the PDF recreates those sections in a print layout rather than capturing browser pixels. Period reports use the applied-period calculation rules and show three separate months per row, with each month showing its interest and principal totals (12 months occupy four rows). Principal/balance details remain in the loan summary and activity sections.

Displayed Loan Manager and PDF dates use MM-DD-YYYY. Stored dates and native date inputs retain ISO format. Period reports identify the report period, number of loans selected, and each loan's borrower, ID, interest rate, origination, scheduled maturity, and period-end outstanding principal. Monthly statements retain their statement header.
